# Onboarding background

Art prompt: preserve the chosen pale-blue aerial horizon, thin clouds and distant coastline; fade into quiet warm white on the left; convey an expansive world and light understanding; remove all text, buttons, logos, cursors, panels and water ripples. Natural daylight, very low contrast and generous negative space. Logical composition: 960×680; raster: 1491×1055.
